# APPLY — produce a branded touchpoint

Use APPLY when the current brand contract is sufficient and a new artifact/touchpoint is needed. Apply the system; do not redesign the brand by default. When the touchpoint is a material visual deliverable, load `visual-artifact-production.md` and produce the actual artifact, seen in its context.

## 1. Compile the smallest sufficient context

Read only what can materially change this artifact: its audience and job, relevant strategy and desired meaning, verbal behavior for the moment, identity grammar and distinctive cues, applicable type/color/composition/imagery rules, production/accessibility/legal constraints, and the real content and native assets. Do not reload unrelated naming, portfolio or historical detail.

## 2. Resolve the artifact job

Infer from the request and available evidence what the artifact must accomplish, its content and hierarchy, medium, dimensions and environment, moment and tone, and the constraints that change execution. Ask only when a missing truth or authority can materially change the artifact.

## 3. Compose from grammar, not logo stamping

Use the thesis and system principles to make the artifact specific to its job, with a hero moment where the signature performs. Preserve family resemblance without copying another layout; a good application may vary substantially from prior work while sharing the brand's generative logic. For a novel or consequential touchpoint, explore materially different compositions internally, then converge.

## 4. Verify

The artifact accomplishes its job; expression comes from the grammar rather than decoration or logo placement; hierarchy supports comprehension and action; tone fits the moment; distinctive cues remain meaningful rather than mechanically repeated; real content did not expose a missing or over-rigid rule; production and accessibility constraints hold. Judge from the rendered artifact in context.

## 5. Fix the right level

A one-off defect is fixed in the artifact. A recurring ambiguity or a failure repeated across legitimate touchpoints becomes an EVOLVE candidate; do not mutate the contract because one execution is weak, and do not keep patching artifacts when repeated exceptions expose a system cause.

## Delivery

The requested artifact, seen in context. Rationale only where it improves future judgment, verification performed and any proposed system change go to the operator, separate from the artifact.
